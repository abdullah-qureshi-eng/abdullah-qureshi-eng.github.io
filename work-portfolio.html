Co-op Work Portfolio

What I do as an Estimator co-op at Aecon and what I'm learning along the way.

Estimator Co-op, Aecon Group Inc.

I'm completing a 12 month co-op (September 2026 to September 2027) in Utilities Estimating at Aecon's Toronto office, reporting to a Senior Estimator. Estimating is the step before construction where a project's quantities, labour and costs get figured out so the company can put in a bid.

What I do

  • Quantity takeoffs in Bluebeam Revu from drawings and specifications
  • Organizing quantities in Excel and building estimate items in InEight Estimate
  • Working through bid packages and the proposal checklist
  • Reviewing past estimates to learn how experienced estimators structure their work

Project names, clients and pricing are left out to respect Aecon's confidentiality.

Highlights so far

Trial estimate on a past project

Sept to Oct 2026

After training modules in InEight and Bluebeam, I built a full trial estimate on a previously submitted project: takeoffs in Bluebeam, quantities organized in Excel, and estimate items such as duct banks built in InEight.

What I learned: Real construction documents don't hand you the problem neatly like a textbook. I had to slow down and understand the scope before measuring anything. My QA/QC habits from Toyota helped me catch units that didn't match.

Use of Engineering ToolsEconomics and Project Management
Why these tags: This was my first time using industry estimating software to turn drawings into a costed estimate.

First live bid

Nov 2026

I was assigned to help a senior estimator on a live bid and completed the cable and communication duct bank takeoffs.

What I learned: Knowing my numbers would actually be used made me check my work more carefully. It also showed me that Bluebeam speed is still my biggest area to improve, which is now one of my goals.

Use of Engineering ToolsProfessionalism
Why these tags: Live bid work means accuracy and deadlines matter, and other people rely on my quantities.

My learning objectives for this work term

  1. Get faster and more accurate at quantity takeoffs in Bluebeam Revu
  2. Learn to navigate long bid packages and find what I need quickly
  3. Become fully independent in InEight Estimate

At the midpoint, InEight is where I've grown the most. I can now build estimate items with much less help. Bluebeam and bid packages are still in progress.
