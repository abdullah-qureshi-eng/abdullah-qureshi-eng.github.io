About Me

I'm a Civil Engineering student at York University's Lassonde School of Engineering, expecting to graduate in July 2028. Right now I'm on a 12 month co-op as an Estimator in Utilities Estimating at Aecon, where I do quantity takeoffs in Bluebeam Revu and build estimates in InEight Estimate. Before that, I spent two terms at Toyota Motor Manufacturing Canada in quality inspection, which taught me how much small details matter. I've always been drawn to skylines and how big projects actually come together, and my goal is to become a licensed Professional Engineer working on major building and infrastructure projects.
